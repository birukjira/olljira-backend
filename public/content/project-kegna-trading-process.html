Kegna Trading operates the largest agricultural equipment assembly facility in Ethiopia — a vast industrial floor where tractors move through assembly stages all day. They needed to see everything, from anywhere, without exception. PTZ coverage for the assembly line Fixed cameras alone cannot track a moving assembly line, so the design centers on PTZ cameras that operators can pan, tilt, and zoom across the full production floor. Camera positions were chosen from the line itself — every workstation and staging area is covered, inside and out. Industrial-grade cabling An assembly plant is a hard environment for cabling: long runs, electrical interference, dust, and vibration. We pulled industrial-grade Ethernet in proper containment back to a rackmount NVR with centralized, redundant storage — so no single disk failure costs the client their footage. Remote monitoring from Addis Ababa Headquarters watches the plant live from Addis Ababa over a secured VPN tunnel. Management can review any camera, any shift, without setting foot on site — and the full system was documented and handed over with a one-year workmanship warranty.
