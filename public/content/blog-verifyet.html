Introducing Verify.ET

A recent Shega Media post reported that 99% of digital transactions among Ethiopia's micro-enterprise merchants are processed through personal accounts, based on the Merchant Payments Observatory Wave 1 survey by FSD Ethiopia and DFS Lab.

EBC also reported that Ethiopia's digital payment system now facilitates more than 18.5 trillion Birr annually.

That is roughly:

18.5 trillion Birr / 365 days = about 50.7 billion Birr per day.

Now take a conservative scenario.

If SMBs and micro merchants account for just 2% of that daily digital payment volume, that is about 1.014 billion Birr moving through small businesses every day. If only 0.5% of that flow is affected by fake receipts, reversed payments, wrong references, scams, or bad actors, that still represents about 5.07 million Birr/day in potential exposure for small merchants. that is 1,850,550,000 birr/year(!!).

This is not a claim that 5 million Birr is being lost every day. It is a simple way to show the size of the risk. When digital payment volume grows this quickly, even tiny failure rates become expensive.

For a large business, one disputed payment might be a support ticket or they may have a payment gateway like Chapa, Santimpay, Arifpay so they wouldn't need verification. but for a small shop, a restaurant, a delivery seller, a freelancer, or a market merchant, it can be the day's profit lost.

That is why we built Verify.ET.

Verify.ET is a transaction verification platform for Ethiopian payments. It helps businesses and individuals confirm whether a payment is real before handing over goods, confirming an order, or closing a sale.

The basic flow is simple:

  1. Select the bank or wallet.
  2. Enter the transaction reference, receipt number, or required payment detail.
  3. Verify the transaction.
  4. Get a clear result you can act on.

No guessing from screenshots or "success" pages from bank mobile apps. No relying only on forwarded SMS messages. No manually opening multiple bank portals when a customer is waiting or waiting for SMS to arrive.

What Verify.ET supports today

  • Real-time transaction verification for supported Ethiopian banks and wallets.
  • Web verification for quick manual checks.
  • A public API for developers and businesses that want verification inside their own systems.

  • Mobile verification for merchants on the go.

Verify Mobile App

The Verify mobile app is built for the way small merchants actually work.

Most payment proof does not arrive as a clean API payload. It comes as a screenshot, a QR code, a receipt image, a Telegram message, or a customer holding up their phone.

The Verify.ET mobile app makes that easy:

  • Scan a live QR code.
  • Take a photo of a receipt.
  • Choose a screenshot or receipt image from the gallery.
  • Let Smart Scan check for QR data first.
  • If there is no usable QR, the app falls back to OCR.
  • When confidence is high, the app prepares the verification automatically.
  • When confidence is lower, the app prefills the fields and lets the user review before verifying.
  • Manual entry still works when scanning is not the right option.

This matters because the fastest tool is the one a merchant can use while serving a customer. Open the app, scan or upload the proof, and verify.


How we addressed Security

Security and durability were part of the foundation, not an afterthought.

Verify.ET is designed to protect user and business data while keeping verification dependable:

  • Authentication is handled through Better Auth sessions, with API key support for integrations.
  • API keys can be scoped with permissions and controlled with rate limits and usage caps.
  • Mobile secrets and session data are stored using secure device storage instead of plain app storage.
  • Production mobile builds require HTTPS backend URLs.
  • Every request gets a request ID for traceability.
  • Errors are standardized so internal details and stack traces are not exposed to users.
  • Logs are structured and sensitive values such as tokens, cookies, passwords, API keys, and receipt/reference data are redacted.
  • Verification requests support idempotency, reducing duplicate processing when a user retries.
  • Slow or busy bank checks can be queued, polled, streamed, or completed through webhook callbacks.
  • Webhook delivery is hardened against unsafe internal/private destinations.
  • Public status and uptime monitoring help users understand whether an issue is with Verify.ET or an upstream bank service.
  • Receipt extraction is designed around short, backend-controlled retention instead of keeping uploaded images forever.

The goal is not only to say "verified" or "not verified." The goal is to make payment confirmation easier, safer, and more reliable for the people who need it most.
