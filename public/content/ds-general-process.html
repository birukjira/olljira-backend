We partnered with DS General PLC to turn a respected construction and import-export company into a recognizable digital brand with a practical publishing engine behind it. The goal was not just to make the company look modern. The goal was to make it easier to trust, easier to search, easier to contact, and much easier for the DS General team to keep current without calling a developer every time a comma needed career growth. Brand foundation Before the website, we rebuilt the identity system. We refined the logo direction, established a sharper blue-led visual language, selected clean typography, and created brand usage patterns that could survive real business life: letterheads, business cards, email templates, notification layouts, employee IDs, and social-ready compositions. In other words, the brand got a suit, a toolkit, and a calendar invite. Digital identity and footprint We translated the new identity into a complete public website at dsgeneralplc.com. The site presents DS General PLC as a major construction and import-export business with clear service pathways, sector pages, project stories, leadership credibility, gallery content, partner signals, contact flows, and career opportunities. Every major page was designed to feel corporate, confident, and readable rather than decorative for decoration's sake. Dynamic CMS architecture Behind the public site, we built a full CMS dashboard. The DS General team can manage blogs, services, business sectors, client projects, products, partners, testimonials, achievements, FAQs, gallery categories, gallery items, organizational structure, newsletters, vacancies, applications, contacts, tags, users, and social links. The content model is not a tiny admin panel wearing a large hat; it is a proper publishing system with structured records, rich text, images, relations, and public/client API surfaces. Engineering details The platform is a modern TypeScript monorepo powered by React 19, Vite, TanStack Router, TanStack Query, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Hono, Drizzle ORM, PostgreSQL, Better Auth, Bun, and Turborepo. The backend is organized into feature modules with controllers, services, repositories, validators, schemas, and routes, making future additions straightforward instead of archaeological. SEO and performance We implemented centralized brand SEO configuration, Open Graph defaults, server-side SEO support, dynamic sitemap generation from live content, robots.txt generation, canonical URL handling, rich metadata, public route mapping, optimized assets, SSR build output, and performance guardrail scripts for chunk size, CSS size, largest asset size, and LCP image size. The result is a website that can be found, shared, indexed, and loaded without making visitors reconsider the internet. Outcome DS General PLC now has a coherent brand identity, consistent business collateral, a professional web presence, and a CMS that lets the company keep momentum after launch. The project created a digital footprint worthy of a company operating across construction, logistics, import-export, and supply-chain-facing work: serious, searchable, maintainable, and polished enough to shake hands before the first meeting.
