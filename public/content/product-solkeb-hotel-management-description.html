Solkeb Hotel runs four restaurants, a full hotel, and a busy front desk — and wanted one system tying them together. We built the software and the network it runs on, so there is exactly one team to call for the whole stack. Restaurant management, from table to kitchen Every table carries a QR code. The guest scans it with their own phone — no app, no waiting — and the digital menu opens for that table, in English, Amharic, or Somali, with photos, prices, ingredients, and preparation times. From the same screen the guest taps Call Waiter, and the waiter dedicated to that table sees the call instantly on the Android app we built for the staff. Orders placed at the table route straight to the right station: food to the kitchen, drinks to the barista, each with the table number attached. No shouted orders, no lost tickets. Billing and payment verification Every bill is verified against the payment itself — any bank transfer or wallet payment is confirmed before the table is closed, so the front desk never chases a slip and the books always match the drawer. Hotel management and in-room ordering Guests book rooms online through the hotel's website at hotel.solkebhotel.com, which we also built, with payments verified the same way as the restaurants. Once checked in, a guest can order from their room — the order lands in the same restaurant system, linked to their booking and their bill. The network underneath All of it runs on the infrastructure we installed: a TP-Link Omada managed network with a configurable router, separate wireless networks for reception and the restaurants, high-speed access points, and instant cloud backup of everything. Guests receive a free Wi-Fi voucher that is tied to the menu — it works while they are dining and expires when they leave, keeping the guest network clean and secure. One system, one team Because we built both sides — the guest menu, the waiter app, the hotel platform, and the network underneath — everything was designed to work together, and Solkeb has a single accountable partner for the entire operation.
