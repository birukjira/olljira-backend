Passport.ET is a fast, open-source passport readiness lookup built for a very human problem: people needed to know whether their Ethiopian passport was ready, where to collect it, and when to go, without decoding PDFs, installing an app, joining a bot, or making a hopeful trip that ends with “come back later.”

The product turns official passport publication data into a simple web experience. Users can search by request number, name, or related fields, browse the latest passports ready for pickup, and view detailed pickup information from any phone with data. That sounds small until you remember how much time, transport cost, uncertainty, and family coordination can sit behind one status check. Passport.ET exists to remove that friction.

Utility Delivered

Since launch, Passport.ET has grown from a useful lookup tool into a recognizable civic-tech utility. The platform has now surpassed more than 1.9 million recorded passport entries, and more than 140,000 people have used it to check readiness information. Today, it continues to grow at roughly 12,000 users per month, serving both people in Ethiopia and family members across the diaspora who help relatives follow up on passport status.

Audience and Reach

The strongest usage comes from Ethiopia, with 8.27k visitors representing 79% of the latest country-level traffic. The product also shows meaningful diaspora and regional usage from Somalia, the United States, South Africa, the Netherlands, the United Arab Emirates, France, Saudi Arabia, the United Kingdom, and Germany. In practice, that means the product is not just helping applicants directly; it is helping families, friends, and communities coordinate around a process that often stretches across cities and borders.

What We Built

Passport.ET combines a public search experience, indexed passport publication data, latest-ready browsing, detailed result pages, admin workflows, and a production data pipeline. The backend was built in the open under the MIT License at https://github.com/NegusNati/passport.et, with PDF-to-database parsing, queued background jobs, automated daily updates, role and permission based admin routes, rate limiting, Chapa integration, and Docker/Traefik deployment. The modern frontend at https://github.com/NegusNati/passport-frontend adds the cleaner React/TanStack experience, Tailwind UI, Web Vitals and PostHog tracking, performance testing, and a search-first interaction model.

The Rebrand and UI/UX Shift

Around eight months ago, we reworked the brand and user experience. That refresh changed the product’s trajectory. The interface became more credible, easier to scan, calmer on mobile, and clearer in the moments that matter: searching, reading a result, and deciding whether it is time to go collect. The product moved from “helpful side project” energy into something closer to a public utility with a recognizable face. Same mission, better posture.

Why It Matters

Passport.ET is useful because it reduces uncertainty. It helps people avoid unnecessary trips, saves time for applicants and families, makes public data more accessible, and gives local businesses a lightweight advertising surface that supports sustainability without forcing users into an app install or a closed channel. The best products are often not the loudest ones. Sometimes they just answer the question everyone keeps asking, quickly and reliably.
