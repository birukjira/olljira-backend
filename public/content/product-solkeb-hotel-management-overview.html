A complete hotel and restaurant platform for Solkeb Hotel in Jijiga — four restaurants on a QR digital menu with table-side waiter calling and a dedicated Android app for waiters, order routing to kitchen and barista, billing verified against any bank or wallet payment, online room booking with in-room ordering, and voucher-based guest Wi-Fi tied to the menu. All of it running on the network we designed and installed for the property ourselves. The guest menu is live at menu.solkebhotel.com — scan any table QR in the restaurant and it opens.
