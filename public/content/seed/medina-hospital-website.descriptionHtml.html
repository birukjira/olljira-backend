What we built

  • Fully dynamic, CMS-driven website — every page editable by hospital staff through an admin panel
  • Find-a-Doctor search with doctor profiles, specialties, and branch assignments
  • Service and department pages for both branches (Kebele 05 and Kebele 07), plus careers and news & events
  • Health-tips blog for patient education and community outreach
  • Research section where the hospital publishes studies and clinical papers
  • Online learning platform with courses and training modules for staff and students
  • Direct contact and booking by phone or Telegram

Why it matters

A 24/7 hospital serves its community around the clock — its website should too. Patients find the right doctor before they travel, families read reliable health guidance instead of rumors, researchers can cite the hospital's published work, and staff keep their skills current through the learning platform. And because the site is fully dynamic, none of this waits on a developer: the hospital's own team publishes news, courses, and pages the moment they're ready.
