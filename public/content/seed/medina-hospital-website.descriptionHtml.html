Medina Primary Hospital is the only private hospital in Jijiga with 24/7 specialist care across two branches — Kebele 07 (maternal & children health) and Kebele 05 (internal medicine, GI & hepatology). They needed a website that works as hard as their front desk: one that patients can actually use, and that the hospital can update itself. Find a Doctor, not a phone number The centerpiece is a real doctor directory. Patients browse specialist profiles — gastroenterology, OB/GYN, pediatrics, internal medicine — filter by service and branch, and reach the right doctor without calling around. Booking happens the way patients in the region actually book: by phone call or Telegram, one tap from any page. Everything is editable The site is fully dynamic. Doctors, services, departments, careers openings, news & events, and health-tip articles are all managed by hospital staff from an admin panel — when a new specialist joins or a branch changes its hours, the site updates in minutes, not after a developer call. The same panel feeds the Patient Portal link that sits in the top navigation of every page. Built to be found Fast-loading pages, proper search-engine structure, and content in the language patients search in — so someone looking for an endoscopy or a pediatrician in Jijiga finds Medina first. Visit it live at mhcs.org.et.
