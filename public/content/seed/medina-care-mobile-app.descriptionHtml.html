What we're building

  • Telemedicine: video consultations with Medina doctors from anywhere
  • Online appointment booking with real-time availability across both branches
  • In-app payments for consultations and services
  • Direct access to lab results and imaging reports from the patient portal
  • Appointment reminders and result notifications on the phone

Why it matters

For patients outside Jijiga, a follow-up consultation can cost a full day of travel. Medina Care brings the hospital to the phone: book in a minute, pay in the app, see the doctor over video, and read your results the moment they're approved. Launching soon on mobile.
