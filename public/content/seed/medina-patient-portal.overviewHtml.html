A secure patient portal for Medina Primary Hospital in Jijiga — patients view their lab results, imaging reports (X-ray, ultrasound, and other scans), procedures, and prescriptions online the moment they're approved, signing in with just their chart number and an SMS code sent to their phone. Results are delivered automatically by SMS and email, and a Telegram bot reads out report links for patients who prefer messaging apps. Every printed report carries a verifiable QR code. Live at portal.mhcs.org.et.
