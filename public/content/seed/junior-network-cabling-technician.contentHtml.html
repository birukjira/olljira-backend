OllJira Technology Solution is looking for a Junior Network Cabling Technician to support our structured cabling crews on projects across Addis Ababa. This is a learning role: you will work alongside certified engineers who test every run, label every port, and document every job — and you will be expected to learn to do the same.

What you will do

  • Assist with cable pulling, containment, and pathway preparation on site
  • Learn termination, keystone and patch-panel work, and rack dressing
  • Support testing with cable analyzers and help label ports at both ends
  • Keep work areas clean and safe in operating offices, clinics, schools, and hotels
  • Help prepare handover documentation and test reports

What we are looking for

  • Technical school background or 6+ months of any hands-on installation experience
  • Willingness to work early mornings, nights, and weekends when clients require it
  • Physical fitness for ladder work and cable pulling
  • Punctuality and honesty — we work unsupervised inside client businesses

What we offer

  • A structured path from junior to lead technician, with certification support
  • Training on Fluke testing, labeling standards, and documentation practice
  • Contract terms with the option to move to full-time based on performance
