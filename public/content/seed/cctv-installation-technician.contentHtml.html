OllJira Technology Solution is hiring a CCTV Installation Technician to join our field team in Addis Ababa. We install Hikvision and Dahua IP surveillance systems for offices, clinics, schools, and hotels — and we hold ourselves to one standard: done once, done right, documented.

What you will do

  • Join site surveys with our lead engineer to map camera positions and cable pathways
  • Mount dome and bullet cameras, pull and terminate Cat6 runs, and dress racks to spec
  • Configure NVRs, retention settings, and remote viewing for clients
  • Test every camera angle with the client before handover and label every device
  • Respond to warranty callouts and scheduled maintenance visits

What we are looking for

  • 1+ year of hands-on experience with CCTV, networking, or electrical installation work
  • Comfort working at height, on ladders, and in ceiling spaces
  • Basic understanding of IP networking (IP addresses, PoE, NVR setup)
  • A careful, tidy working style — clients invite us into operating businesses
  • Hikvision HCSA certification is a strong plus; we will support the right candidate to earn it

What we offer

  • Full-time employment with a certified, growing field team
  • Certification support (Hikvision HCSA, MikroTik MTCNA)
  • All tools, test equipment, and safety gear provided
  • Real documentation practices you will be proud to put your name on
