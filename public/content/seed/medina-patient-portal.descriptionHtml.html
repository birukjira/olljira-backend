Before the portal, a Medina Primary Hospital patient who wanted a lab result had one option: travel back to the hospital, wait at the records desk, and carry a paper printout home. For a hospital serving two branches and patients across the Somali region, that meant queues at the desk and delayed care. We built the Medina Patient Portal to put those records in the patient's pocket. Results the moment they're ready Lab results appear in the portal as soon as the laboratory approves them — blood tests, imaging reports (X-ray, ultrasound, ECG, endoscopy), procedures, and prescriptions. No return trip, no waiting for printouts. Login that works for everyone No passwords to forget, no email required. The patient enters the chart number printed on their hospital card, receives a one-time SMS code on their phone, and they're in. An email + password option is there for those who prefer it. It's authentication designed for real patients, not for IT departments. Verified, secure reports Every report the portal produces carries a QR code that anyone — a referral hospital, an employer, an embassy — can scan to verify the document is genuine and untampered. Patient data stays server-side and access is logged. The portal links directly from the hospital's main website, which we also built, so patients move between the two without friction. A Medina Care mobile app built on the same platform is coming next. Try it at portal.mhcs.org.et.
