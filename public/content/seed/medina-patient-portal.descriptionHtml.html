What we built

  • Secure sign-in with chart number + one-time SMS code — no passwords for patients to forget
  • Lab results, procedures, and prescriptions online the moment they're approved
  • Imaging reports — X-ray, ultrasound, and other scans — viewable and downloadable in the portal
  • Automatic result notifications by SMS and email
  • Telegram bot that reads the website and delivers report links to patients on Telegram
  • Verifiable QR code on every printed report

Why it matters

Before the portal, collecting results meant a second trip to the hospital and a wait at the records desk — hard for patients traveling from outside Jijiga. Now the result finds the patient: an SMS or email lands the moment it's approved, Telegram users get their link from the bot, and anyone who needs the paper copy can verify it instantly by scanning the QR code.
