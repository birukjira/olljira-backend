A fully dynamic website for Medina Primary Hospital in Jijiga — doctor profiles and a Find-a-Doctor search, service and department pages for both branches (Kebele 05 and Kebele 07), careers, news & events, health tips, and direct booking by phone or Telegram. Everything on the site is managed by the hospital's own staff through an admin panel — no developer needed for day-to-day updates. Live at mhcs.org.et.
