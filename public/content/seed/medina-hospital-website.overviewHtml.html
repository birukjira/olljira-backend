A fully dynamic website for Medina Primary Hospital in Jijiga — doctor profiles and a Find-a-Doctor search, service and department pages for both branches (Kebele 05 and Kebele 07), careers, news & events, and a health-tips blog. A dedicated research section publishes the hospital's own studies and clinical papers, and an online learning platform lets staff and students take courses and training modules directly on the site. Visitors can reach the hospital by phone or Telegram, and everything is managed by the hospital's own staff through an admin panel — no developer needed for day-to-day updates. Live at mhcs.org.et.
